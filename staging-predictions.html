How PP are calculated

S = 0.85A + 0.15 × min(max(H, A), 1.5A)
PP = 100 × S / Smax

A is average DECODE OPR, floored at zero. H is highest DECODE OPR. Smax is the largest strength score among all San Diego teams with recorded DECODE OPR in the frozen catalog.

  • Average performance supplies 85% of the signal.
  • The peak contribution is capped at 1.5× average OPR. An exceptional event can add at most 7.5% to average-based strength.
  • The top team receives 100 PP. A roster sums the displayed PP of its teams.
  • No DECODE OPR means unscored, not zero. A roster containing an unscored team has no complete prediction.

PP is a comparison index, not a predicted match score or earned fantasy points. The weights have not been fitted or backtested. No BioBuzz results, prices, awards, or rank bonuses affect the score.
