These preseason estimates use DECODE’s scoring scale. They are not earned points or a validated numerical forecast for BioBuzz.

  • Event histories let us compare recent performance, improvement, and the amount of supporting evidence.
  • Teams with only summary data use their historical average. Missing data remains unscored.
  • OPR is already an estimated scoring contribution. Pollen cost is used only to compare value.
  • A season scenario repeats each team’s per-event estimate for the event count you enter. Attendance and future improvement are not assumed.

The historical test predicts later DECODE events using only earlier results. Its mean error is not a confidence interval or an accuracy percentage.

15 Pollen budget · Choose 1–3 teams

Pollen cost is the whole-number price taken from your budget. PP is projected performance and can exceed 15. Prices are 3, 4, 5, 6, or 11 Pollen.

  • Premium team + support: 11 + 4 = 15
  • Two strong picks + budget pick: 6 + 6 + 3 = 15
  • Three mid-tier picks: 5 + 5 + 5 = 15
